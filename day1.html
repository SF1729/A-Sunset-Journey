Isliye ye poore days ka plan banaya hai. Pata hai, thoda extra hai... par chal, tere liye itna toh banta hai. 🤌🏻😭

Honestly, mujhe nahi pata tujhe ye sab dekh ke kaisa lagega. Tu emotional hoga ya phir, as always, koi farak hi nahi padega. 😒 Khair...

Lekin jo bhi ho, maine ye sab apni taraf se poori effort se banaya hai. 🧡

Apni friendship mein bohot saari cheezein hoti hain. Hasi-mazaak hota hai, kabhi arguments bhi hote hain. Kabhi ek doosre ki taang kheenchte hain, kabhi bina matlab ke pareshan karte hain.

Thank you, Budduu, meri life ka part hone ke liye. 💛🫂

Toh aaram se enjoy kar, aur haan, next day unlock karne ke chakkar mein koi setting mat karna. Sab apne time pe khulega. 😭🔒

Happy Birthday in advance, Budduu! 💛🌅
